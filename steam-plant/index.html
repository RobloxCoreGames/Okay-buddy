Operator notes

Lamp colours follow North American power-plant practice. Red means running, energized or closed (breakers). Green means stopped or open. Amber means tripped or in alarm.

The burner management system enforces the NFPA 85 sequence: purge, then pilot, then main flame. A flame lost for 3 s trips the master fuel valve. Natural gas that collects in a hot furnace will find an ignition source.

When firing rises, the drum level first swells as bubbles grow under the surface, then falls as water boils away. Three-element control (level, steam flow and feed flow) handles this above about 25 % load. Trust the gauge glass over a transmitter.

Before rolling the turbine, open the drains to warm the main steam line. Condensate slugs cause water hammer. Pass quickly through the critical speeds and keep vacuum below 10 kPa abs. To synchronize, match frequency, voltage and phase, then close at 12 o'clock.
